# Icon Design & IP Compliance Audit

This document records the provenance and intellectual property (IP) compliance process for the "Rooney" application icon.

## 1. Overview & Provenance
- **Asset:** Application Icon (`images/Rooney-matte-icon.svg`) and Branding Assets for "Rooney" (High-Performance Split-View Editor for COSMIC / Wayland).
- **Generation Method:** AI-assisted synthesis (via Google Gemini) iteratively refined to eliminate trademark compliance issues and ensure absolute structural originality.
- **Style:** Flat, non-glossy, matte finish on a solid charcoal single-color background, compliant with modern Linux desktop environment (DE) design languages.

## 2. Competitive Visual Search & IP Clearance (As of 2026)
An image-based visual search and trademark policy review were performed to mitigate collision risks with existing OSS projects and software suites.

| Target / Brand | Design Comparison | Risk Assessment & Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Rust Programming Language (Official Logo)** | Previous draft utilized a prominent gear motif with a white-etched "R" character. | **Resolved (Zero Risk)**: The original gear-shaped draft posed an extreme risk of trademark dilution under the Rust Foundation Trademark Policy. The asset has been entirely re-designed. The final icon completely eliminates the gear motif, replacing it with a geometric line monogram that has no correlation with the Rust language identity. |
| **RStudio / Posit** | Developer IDE using an "R" letter inside a solid blue circle. | **Clear Distinction**: "Rooney" utilizes a dark, uniform, single-color charcoal background. The glyph is a stylized geometric monogram representing dual-pane code/preview structures, completely distinguishing it from RStudio's corporate branding. |

## 3. Final Compliance & Technical Architecture Integration
- **Originality (The Blueprint):** The finalized monogram is a custom fusion of three core architectural elements unique to this app: the application headletter "**R**", the internal "**Rope**" buffer concept, and the functional feature of "**Split-View / Two-Pane Editing**".
- **OS/DE Guidelines Compliance:** The flat design language, absence of fake light reflections, and non-gradients perfectly fit the Pop!_OS COSMIC Desktop style.
- **Copyright Status:** Approved for open-source publication under the MIT License within the Rooney project. No third-party copyrights or trademarked silhouettes were used or embedded.
